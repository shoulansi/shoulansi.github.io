# About Me

When I first entered the workforce, I started in different food and retail jobs. Over time I moved into management roles at Dollar General and Domino’s, where I developed patience, consideration, leadership skills, and a strong work ethic.

My interest in tech started with hardware. Building computers, tinkering with parts, things like that. With help from my cousin, I took the leap and started learning code. That decision set me on a whole new path, and now I'm focused on becoming a full-stack developer.

---

## Who I Am

I'm a quick learner and a hard worker. I enjoy problem-solving and I like figuring things out. I’m looking for a role in tech where I can continue growing, contribute to a team, and keep improving my skills.

---

## My Values

These are values I try to stick to in everything I do:

- **Honesty** – Always telling the truth. There’s no need to ever lie.
- **Willingness to Learn** – I’m open to learning any new subject. I take in new information constantly.
- **Confidence** – I believe in the code I write and the projects I present.
- **Kindness** – It can be as simple as holding a door or saying something encouraging.
- **Humility** – If I’m wrong, I own it. Ego doesn’t help anyone.
- **Sincerity** – Say what you mean. Mean what you say.

---

## My Goals

My main goal is to become the best developer I can be. I want to push my skills to the limit, take on real projects, and work with a team that supports growth and creativity.
